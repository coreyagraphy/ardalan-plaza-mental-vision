- West pavilion addresses **4th Ave SW** (the corner).
- North facade addresses **Main Street** toward 3rd Ave SW.
- Fountain = roundabout island. Not a pocket plaza in front of the storefronts.
- Brick Arts & Design District sits **across Main** (north) and **across 3rd** (east).
- Nothing plugs the west side of Ardalan except sidewalk + 4th Ave + the roundabout.
